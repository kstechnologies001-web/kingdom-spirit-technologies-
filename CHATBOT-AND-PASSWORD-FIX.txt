CHATBOT AND PASSWORD RECOVERY NOTES

CHATBOT
- The browser widget is loaded from the root-level chatbot.js and styled by root-level chatbot.css.
- The backend endpoint is POST /api/chat, routed by the root _worker.js.
- Cloudflare Pages must have a Workers AI binding named exactly AI.
- The chatbot reads public.faq_entries rows where is_published = true. Keep private or draft information unpublished.
- If the AI binding is missing, the launcher may still appear, but AI responses will show a setup/support message.

PASSWORD RECOVERY
- The forgot-password flow sends Supabase Auth emails to /reset-password.html.
- The reset page and both recovery scripts use the root-level path. There is no required /account/ folder.
- In Supabase Auth URL Configuration, allow the deployed site's wildcard Redirect URL.
- Confirm that the reset link opens the deployed website, then update the password and sign in again.
